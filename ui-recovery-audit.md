# UI Recovery Audit — August 2026

## Confirmed regressions

| Surface | Observed issue | Recovery direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile footer | A large paper-coloured gap appears after the ink footer because global app-frame safe-area padding sits outside the footer surface. | Remove global trailing padding and absorb dock clearance inside the navy footer. |
| Home hero | Desktop hero leaves a large unarticulated pale area and its atlas object reads too faintly. | Restore a clearer two-column composition with a visible KA compass, date marker, paper/thread motif, and bounded calendar object. |
| Primary imagery | Mountain/landscape photography dominates the Field Window and season card, weakening festival specificity. | Replace the dominant travel treatment with paper, thread, stamp, textile, and calendar-object materials; retain imagery only as restrained contextual texture. |
| Header and route rhythm | Header remains usable, but several route openings have excessive blank top/right space relative to their content. | Normalize desktop frames and hero paddings; retain attached header, quiet navigation, and mobile dock. |
| Mobile route endings | Home, Index, Year, Guide, Season, and detail all expose the footer gap; full-route content otherwise remains readable. | Apply shared footer/safe-area repair and retain clear mobile stacks. |

## Recovery baseline

Preserve the paper/ink atlas system, active saffron date signals, terracotta thread details, readable bilingual hierarchy, country/date/place state, and the neutral crystal dock. Avoid adding another component system; use a final recovery layer to consolidate visual priorities, reduce generic imagery, and correct the page-edge/safe-area flaw.

## Desktop recovery result

The Home opening now has a balanced, unobstructed title and date control alongside a bounded paper/thread atlas panel. The Field Window no longer depends on the mountain travel image and instead presents a calm ink, teal, terracotta, and paper calendar material surface. The Solar Ledger, day board, season cards, Year arc, Guide, Season Fieldbook, detail record, and constellation footer remain visible within a consistent paper-and-ink frame.

## Responsive recovery result

At 375px, Home, Index, Year, Guide, Season, and the Raksha Bandhan record use readable single-column or intentional horizontal-scroll layouts. The trailing paper gap below the constellation footer was traced to an inherited mobile footer margin and has been removed. At tablet width, the annual month arc, place selectors, detail story, season model, and field panels remain present without cropped text or broken state controls. Calendar/date/country/place behavior was not changed by the recovery styles.
