# KA Festivals cultural-atlas research notes

## Typography direction

Instrument Serif is positioned by Google Fonts as a modern Scotch-influenced display serif with vintage and sincere qualities. It remains appropriate for KA Festivals’ large editorial headlines and the refined wordmark, while a separate utility sans and mono face should carry search, filters, dates, labels, and bilingual controls. The refinement will add **Manrope** for soft humanist body and interface text while retaining **DM Mono** for archival metadata; this keeps large typography expressive without cluttering practical controls.

Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Serif

## Footer constellation direction

The footer uses Pisces and Aquarius as decorative sky-navigation marks rather than astrology. NASA Chandra’s Aquarius note places Aquarius among nearby water-related constellations including Pisces and describes a longstanding association with water imagery across cultures. The footer will translate this into two understated, labelled star paths and a seasonal “water routes” motif, with no predictive or zodiac claims.

Source: https://chandra.harvard.edu/photo/constellations/aquarius.html
