# KA Festivals Enhancement Audit — August 2026

## Baseline assessment

The existing site is coherent, functional, and already aligned with its warm paper, deep-ink, editorial field-atlas identity. The enhancement pass will preserve the current routes, data, bilingual flow, calendar state, seasonal model, and restrained crystal navigation rather than redesigning them.

## Accepted refinement contract

| Area | Observed opportunity | Enhancement direction |
|---|---|---|
| KA identity | The compass bloom, route spine, date signals, and stamps read as separate motifs on some routes. | Establish a small shared coordinate system so page headers, focus blocks, and notices use consistent KA field labels, route rules, and status metadata. |
| Paper versus night ink | The dark panels are attractive but can outweigh the reading surface on the daily companion. | Keep night ink exclusive to the solar instrument, selected evidence, and sky footer; introduce quieter paper-field treatment around dense reading sections. |
| Festival specificity | Existing visual choices are stable but several story surfaces rely on broad atmosphere. | Add labeled atlas-object cues and contextual captions around visual/story areas rather than introducing uncontrolled new imagery. |
| Guide utility page | The equal three-card layout is comparatively generic. | Add an editorial route rail, clearer field numbering, and asymmetrical annotations while retaining its compact content structure. |
| Feedback and accessibility | Existing controls benefit from more visible keyboard focus, pressed feedback, and consistent interactive affordances. | Add a final shared enhancement layer for focus-visible outlines, active press states, motion restraint, hover/touch feedback, and reduced-motion behavior. |

## Guardrails

The enhancement must retain the attached desktop header, neutral crystal mobile dock, warm paper base, verified/estimated/review date distinctions, culture-guide caveats, selected date/country/month continuity, and the scope statement that modeled daylight and seasons are not live weather.
