# Nature Glass Visual Direction

The supplied references establish two useful but deliberately bounded ideas for KA Festivals. The first is **layered calm**: a precise, lightly translucent information pane with a narrow border and clear typography, sitting over an image rather than competing with it. The second is **contained atmosphere**: deep botanical imagery provides a single immersive moment, while the information remains in a softly blurred, high-contrast foreground card.

KA Festivals will use these ideas in one focused calendar feature rather than across every page. The selected-date area will become a botanical **Field Window** with a low-key nature image, a darkened safety overlay, and a glass calendar summary. The rest of the product will retain its warm paper atlas materials. This preserves hierarchy, prevents a generic dashboard aesthetic, and ensures that dates, status labels, and bilingual text remain readable.

The shared header will gain a quieter frosted travel-strip quality, while the footer will receive measured editorial rails rather than more glass effects. On mobile, the floating dock will retain translucent depth but avoid narrow, unreadable controls. All blur layers will be treated as progressive enhancement and paired with opaque fallbacks for contrast and performance.
