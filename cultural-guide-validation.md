# Cultural guide validation

The guide was checked on researched Indian and Indonesian details at desktop and 375-pixel mobile widths. Raksha Bandhan, Idul Fitri, and Nyepi display a clear field note, the existing status context, a collection visual, and then the new **How the day is often celebrated** block. The new guide has a recognisable meaning statement, three short example moments, a visitor-facing regional/etiquette note, and a source link where a reviewed institutional source supports the more detailed content.

Mobile screenshots show the full Indonesian guide without horizontal clipping. Headline copy, bullet list, source note, next-record link, and constellation footer retain a single readable reading order. The selected collection-image URLs all returned `200` from Pexels and are lazy loaded; their captions state that they are context illustrations rather than one-documentary representations of a celebration.

The specific-guide mapping covers repeating high-recognition festival keys across future calendar years, while every remaining record receives an explicit category-level guide. Date-status labels remain untouched and independent from cultural-practice content.
