# Precision Refinement Audit — August 2026

## Confirmed desktop findings

The existing field-atlas identity remains strong. The user’s requested corrections map to concrete visible issues: the Today hero still has an over-wide light control shelf that stops early; the field-window calendar needs a more legible, centred information plane; Solar Ledger place tabs use an overly broad saffron selected block; the empty-date card relies on the legacy cat icon; and the footer has greater vertical mass than the content preceding it.

The Index month sections need a clearer month label and a more deliberate break from the repeated ledger rows. Timeline is mostly centred at desktop width, but its top feature card, month rail, and lower “Atlas markers” pair need a unified max-width and clearer plain-language legend. Guide’s composition is successful but benefits from additional explanatory content. Season needs a fuller, brighter reference experience without making unqualified weather claims.

## Guardrails adopted

| Area | Accepted direction |
| --- | --- |
| Header and logo | Preserve the mark; use a larger display “KA” plus a smaller, contrasting “Festivals” word in a new font pairing. |
| Controls | Use a slim, blurred crystal selection treatment with coral/saffron detail, never opaque blue or an oversized yellow fill. |
| Today and Solar | Retain the field window and live solar model; improve hierarchy, safe geometry, moon/dusk representation, and plain-language labels. |
| Index and Year | Maintain existing routes/data; centre each main rail and use genuine month/section hierarchy rather than new dashboard cards. |
| Copy | Keep Indonesian default and English complete; shorten labels and explain date/season status in casual, qualified language. |
| Footer motion | Use subtle, reduced-motion-safe opacity/transform stars within the footer, with no heavy canvas, video, or continuous high-cost particle system. |

## Validation focus

Recheck desktop and 375px mobile after implementation. Test the selected date/country/place, locale toggle, festival detail links, calendar deep links, and navigation scroll-reset behavior.

## Post-refinement visual check

At 375px, the wordmark now keeps KA visibly larger than Festivals while retaining compact language/install controls. The crystal dock shows one bounded active capsule and a short coral route marker; each of its four translated labels remains visible. Today’s hero, calendar input, Field Window, Solar Ledger, empty-date signal, season links, Year filter, horizontal month arc, Guide reading path, and Season Fieldbook have no visible clipping in the captured routes.

The annual month arc remains deliberately horizontal on narrow screens, so a visitor can swipe through all twelve months without a compressed or overlapping line. Desktop retains centred annual/atlas rails and a reduced-height footer. Footer stars and meteors use transform/opacity only and are disabled when reduced motion is requested.
